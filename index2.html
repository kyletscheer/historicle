How to Play

Guess the historical date in 6 tries.

Each guess must be a valid date in MM/DD/YYYY format.

After each guess, the color of the tiles will show how close your guess was.

Green: The digit is correct and in the right position.

Yellow: The digit is in the date but in the wrong position.

Gray: The digit is not in the date.

Hints

After 2 guesses, you can use up to 3 hints (one per turn):

Season Hint: Shows the quarter of the year

Event Hint: Gives a clue about what happened

When Hint: Tells if the date is before or after your last guess

Game Modes

Daily Mode: One puzzle per day, shared by everyone

Random Mode: Play unlimited puzzles anytime
